## 2. THE PROBLEM

### Facial Recognition is Broken

#### Bias Crisis
- **91%** of facial recognition systems show racial bias (NIST Study 2023)
- **34% higher** false positive rates for women of color
- **€20M+** in fines for discriminatory AI in EU (2024)

#### Compliance Nightmare
- **EU AI Act** (2024): High-risk system requirements = 93 compliance criteria
- **GDPR**: 72-hour breach notification, €20M fines
- **EAA**: Accessibility mandates for 2025

#### Trust Deficit
- **67%** of EU citizens distrust facial recognition (Eurobarometer 2024)
- **Municipal bans**: 40+ cities prohibit law enforcement use
- **Corporate risk**: Reputational damage from AI scandals

### Current Solutions Are Inadequate
- ❌ **Post-hoc audits**: Discover problems after harm is done
- ❌ **Opaque systems**: Black-box algorithms with no oversight
- ❌ **No human control**: Automated decisions without veto power
- ❌ **Fragmented compliance**: Manual mapping to regulations
